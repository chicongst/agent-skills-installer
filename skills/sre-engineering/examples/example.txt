# Example: /sre-engineering (Mode A — incident response)

## User Input
Our payment processing is failing. Orders are being placed but payments aren't
going through. Started about 20 minutes ago. Error rate on /api/checkout is at
45%. Some orders showing "payment pending" forever. Black Friday campaign started
2 hours ago — traffic is 4x normal. No recent deploys.

## Skill Output

# Incident Report: Checkout payments failing during Black Friday traffic

**Status**: INVESTIGATING
**Severity**: SEV-1 — the checkout/payment journey is failing for 45% of /api/checkout requests at peak campaign traffic
**Started**: ~20 minutes before this report (exact time unknown — confirm from metrics)
**Incident commander**: unassigned — assign now
**Next update**: in 15 minutes (SEV-1 cadence)

## Impact
- **Users affected**: ~45% of /api/checkout requests fail; number of customers unknown — ask
- **Services affected**: /api/checkout; payment processing; order status (orders stuck in "payment pending")
- **Business impact**: payments failing during the Black Friday campaign at 4x normal traffic; revenue figure unknown — ask
- **Data integrity risk**: orders are created but stay "payment pending"; unknown whether any of these customers were actually charged. Retrying or cancelling them in bulk before that is known could double-charge or drop paid orders
- **Trend**: unknown — ask (only one reading, 45%, was given)

## Hypotheses
| # | Hypothesis | Status | Evidence for / against | Next check | Owner |
|---|------------|--------|------------------------|------------|-------|
| 1 | The payment dependency (external provider or internal payment service) is throttling, rejecting, or timing out under 4x load | Open | For: order creation succeeds while the payment step fails; traffic is 4x normal. Against: nothing in the report yet | Break down payment-call outcomes since T−30 min by response code / timeout; check the provider's status page and any quota or rate-limit responses | unassigned |
| 2 | A resource in the payment path is saturated (connection pool, worker pool, queue) and requests hang | Open | For: 4x traffic; "payment pending forever" fits hung or timed-out calls; failures began ~100 min after the ramp, consistent with gradual saturation (assumption). Against: nothing in the report yet | Compare payment-path latency, pool utilisation, and queue depth now vs. before the campaign | unassigned |
| 3 | A non-deploy change around T−20 min (config, feature flag, credential/certificate, provider-side change) | Open | For: onset does not line up with the campaign start 2 h earlier. Against: "no recent deploys" — but that rules out code only | Review config, flag, infra, and secret change logs for the last 3 h; ask the payment provider about changes | unassigned |

## Mitigations
| Option | Use if | Risk | Status |
|--------|--------|------|--------|
| Rate-limit or queue new checkouts at the edge so the payment path stays under its limit | H1 or H2 confirmed | Some customers are turned away or delayed instead of failing; needs an existing throttle mechanism — ask whether one exists | Proposed |
| Add capacity to the saturated component in the payment path | H2 confirmed and the bottleneck is local | Won't help if the limit is the provider or a shared database; more connections can overload a shared database | Proposed |
| Revert the change found in the change logs | H3 confirmed | Low if the change is cleanly reversible | Proposed |
| Ask the campaign owner to pause further traffic drivers (emails, pushes, ads) | Load confirmed as the driver (H1 or H2) | Business cost; campaign owner decides | Proposed |
